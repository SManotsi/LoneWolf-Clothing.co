Returns & exchanges

  • Items must be unworn, unwashed and have all tags attached.
  • Start a return from the Contact page within 30 days of delivery.
  • Refunds go back to the original payment method once the return arrives.
  • For a different size, tell us in your return request and we'll swap it where stock allows.

Caring for your pieces

Wash cold, inside out, with like colours. Hang to dry where you can; it keeps the graphics sharp and the fit true. Each product page lists its own care notes.
