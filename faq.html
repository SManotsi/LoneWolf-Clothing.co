Good to know

Questions, answered.

Quick answers about shopping, delivery, sizing and this demo store. For anything else, send us a note.

Ordering

Can I place a real order on this website?

No. This is a front-end demo. The catalogue, bag and checkout use sample data, and checkout does not take payments or submit an order.

What does the R price marker mean?

Prices are fictional examples, with R used as a sample rand marker. They are not live prices or a promise of availability.

How does the shopping bag work?

Choose a colour and size on a product page (or use Quick add), then review your bag from any page. Your bag and wishlist are saved in this browser using local storage.

Is there a discount code?

For the demo, try LONEWOLF10 at checkout for 10% off.

Delivery & returns

How much is delivery?

Standard delivery is R80, and free over R1,000. Express is R150. See Shipping & returns for sample timings.

What is the returns policy?

In a live store, unworn items with tags could be returned within 30 days. These are sample terms for the demo.

Fit & style

How do your pieces fit?

Most pieces have a relaxed fit. Check the size guide; between sizes, size down for a closer fit or up for extra room.

Where can I find style inspiration?

Visit the Lookbook and use the category filters to browse sample outfit imagery.

How do I ask a question?

Use the Contact page to try the sample form. Its response is a local demo confirmation and does not send your message to a real inbox.
